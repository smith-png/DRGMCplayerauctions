## 2026-05-17 - Authorization Bypass in API\n**Vulnerability:** IDOR / Authorization Bypass in placeBid endpoint.\n**Learning:** The placeBid endpoint previously did not check if the requester had permissions to place a bid on behalf of the team specified in the request.\n**Prevention:** Ensure that critical actions verifying ownership check both the authorization context (e.g. JWT claims via req.user) and the input parameters.
