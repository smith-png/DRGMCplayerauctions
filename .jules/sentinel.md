## 2024-05-24 - [Missing Authorization on sensitive placeBid endpoint]
**Vulnerability:** Found an IDOR/Missing Authorization vulnerability in the `/api/auction/bid` endpoint (`placeBid` function) where any authenticated user (regardless of role or team assignment) could submit a bid on behalf of another team, draining their budget and compromising the auction integrity.
**Learning:** Sensitive business logic endpoints, like placing bids in an auction, must perform explicit authorization checks beyond just verifying the presence of an authentication token. Even if an endpoint uses `authenticateToken`, we must verify that the user is authorized to perform the specific action on the specific target object (e.g. `req.user.team_id == teamId`).
**Prevention:** Always check both Authentication (Who is this?) AND Authorization (Are they allowed to do this to that resource?). Implement strict role-based access control and object-level permission checks inside controller logic for sensitive operations.
