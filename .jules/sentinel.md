## 2026-07-01 - Authorization Bypass in Auction Bidding\n**Vulnerability:** The `placeBid` endpoint (`/api/auction/bid`) allows any authenticated user (even a viewer or participant) to place bids on behalf of any team by simply providing that team's ID.\n**Learning:** Relying solely on `authenticateToken` without verifying the user's role and authorization to act on behalf of a specific resource (like a team) leads to Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) and unauthorized actions.\n**Prevention:** Always verify that the authenticated user has the correct permissions (e.g., admin or team owner) AND that they are authorized to manipulate the specific resource (e.g., ensuring `req.user.team_id === teamId`).
