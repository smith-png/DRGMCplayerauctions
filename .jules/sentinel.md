## 2024-05-24 - Missing Authorization check on Bidding Endpoint\n**Vulnerability:** The placeBid endpoint lacked authorization checks, allowing any authenticated user to place bids on behalf of any team by simply modifying the teamId in the request.\n**Learning:** Relying solely on `authenticateToken` middleware without validating the user authorization details against the request payloads can result in horizontal privilege escalation.\n**Prevention:** Implement strict ownership validation or verify `req.user.team_id` when performing actions on behalf of a specific object/team.
