## 2024-03-15 - [CRITICAL] Fix IDOR in placeBid endpoint
**Vulnerability:** The `/bid` endpoint lacked proper authorization checks to ensure that a team owner could only place a bid for their assigned team. The endpoint only used `authenticateToken` but not `authorizeRoles`, meaning any authenticated user could call it, and even a valid `team_owner` could supply any `teamId` in the request body to place a bid on behalf of another team (Insecure Direct Object Reference).
**Learning:** Endpoints that handle sensitive actions and rely on an ID from the request body (like `teamId`) must actively enforce resource ownership validation, rather than trusting the client-provided ID. The generic `authenticateToken` middleware only verifies JWT validity, not authorization for specific resources.
**Prevention:** Always validate that the `req.user` owns or has explicit permission to manipulate the resource identified by the request parameters or body. For team operations, verify that `req.user.team_id` matches the requested `teamId`, or that the user has an administrative role (`admin` or `auctioneer`).
